# Senior Backend Engineer — Payments Reliability

**Stripe · San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)**

Stripe's Payments Reliability group owns the systems that decide whether a
charge succeeds. We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to raise the
reliability and latency of the core charge path.

## What you'll do

- Own services on the critical charge path with strict latency SLOs (p99 under
  150ms) and five-nines availability targets.
- Design idempotent, exactly-once processing for payments events flowing
  through Kafka.
- Operate Postgres at scale: sharding, replication, and query planning for
  ledger-grade correctness.
- Lead incident response and raise the on-call bar across the group.
- Mentor engineers and drive design reviews for distributed systems changes.

## What we're looking for

- 5+ years building backend systems in Go or Ruby.
- Deep experience with payments infrastructure, distributed systems, and
  observability tooling.
- Comfort with Kubernetes-based deploys and gRPC service meshes.
- A track record of reducing latency and eliminating classes of failure.

Stripe builds economic infrastructure for the internet. Reliability is the
product.
